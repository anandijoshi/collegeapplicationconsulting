FAQ

Good Questions

When should I start?

The earlier the better. Many students begin the summer before senior year, but I work with students at every stage, including the last few weeks before a deadline.

Do you work with transfer students?

Yes. I transferred to Berkeley from community college myself, so I can help with transfer applications as well as freshman applications.

Why do I book a call first?

So we can talk through your goals, see if we’re a good fit, and determine whether my resources and approach would be helpful for you.

How do I sign up for a package?

After our call, if we determine it's a good fit to work together, I'll send you a secure payment invoice.

How does essay review work?

Each week, we’ll have a 1-hour 1-on-1 video call to discuss your application, brainstorm ideas, and work through revisions together. Once you submit your revisions, I’ll provide detailed written feedback within 48 hours. Each week includes one revision round to keep the process focused and intentional.

Do you guarantee admission?

No. Nobody honestly can. What I can promise is a clear strategy, honest feedback, and an application that represents you well.

What if I need to reschedule?

Life happens. You can reschedule from your calendar invite. Please give at least 24 hours' notice when you can.
